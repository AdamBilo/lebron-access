Privacy Policy: LeBron Personal Access

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Who uses it

LeBron Personal Access is a personal, non-commercial app with exactly one user: the person who built it. It signs in only to his own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (his own mailbox): reading, labelling and organising messages, and writing drafts or sending email only when he asks.
  • Google Sheets and Drive (his own files): reading and updating his own spreadsheets.
  • His basic profile (name and email address), to confirm the right account is signed in.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the tasks he asks for, on his own computer. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any other person or company.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on his own computer. No Google user data is kept on any server operated for this app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub page.
